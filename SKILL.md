---
name: wikipedia-interest-research
description: Measures and compares audience interest in topics across Wikipedia language editions using Wikimedia pageview data, judges how trustworthy a trend is, and produces charts plus a one-page PDF report. Use when a user wants to know whether interest in a topic is growing, compare interest between languages or markets, choose a course topic or a localization language for a B2C product, or asks about Wikipedia pageviews — even if they do not mention Wikipedia explicitly.
license: MIT
compatibility: Requires uv (https://docs.astral.sh/uv/) and network access to wikimedia.org and wikipedia.org.
---
