### Final answer

Wikipedia was founded by **Jimmy Wales** and **Larry Sanger** on **January 15, 2001**. 

The project emerged from an earlier experiment called Nupedia, which was an attempt to create an online encyclopedia using expert writers. Wikipedia took a different approach by allowing anyone to contribute and edit, which became the key to its rapid growth and widespread adoption.
